THE OVERVIEW

From a targeted search to organized contact data.

Finding relevant contacts means handling more than a single search response. This application connects company and role searches with profile filtering, pagination, and structured exports through the Unipile API.

INSIDE THE PROJECT

What I worked on.

01

Targeted searches

Built company and employee search with separate company and role keywords using C#/.NET and the Unipile API.

02

Reliable data handling

Implemented cursor-based pagination, profile filtering, and API requests for email, phone, current company, and job-title data.

03

Structured exports

Parsed JSON responses into C# models and exported qualified results to CSV.

04

Control over long queries

Added cancellation and search-stop logic so long-running queries could be interrupted.
